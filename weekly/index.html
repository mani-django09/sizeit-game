A new theme every Monday

The Dailies are quick. The Weekly Challenge is the one to save for a tea break. Every Monday a new theme starts, such as India Week, Space Week, Animal Week, Big Builds, Sports or Everyday, and it brings ten mixed rounds built from that theme.

You only get one go each week, and everyone playing that week gets the same ten rounds, so it's a fair fight when you compare scores.

The four kinds of round

  • 📏 Size: which of two things is taller, longer or wider?
  • ⚖️ Weight: which of two things is heavier?
  • 🏁 Speed: which of two things is faster?
  • 🔢 Ballpark: which range does a real-world number fall in?

Each right answer is worth a point, for ten in all. Press 1 to 4 to answer and Enter to move on.

The six themes

The themes take turns, one per week, so each comes round every six weeks with a fresh set of rounds.

  • 🇮🇳 India Week: monuments, cricket, autos and tigers. The Qutub Minar, the Statue of Unity, the Vande Bharat Express and a cricket pitch all turn up.
  • 🚀 Space Week: planets, light, orbits and the speed of sound, from the International Space Station to the Earth's trip around the Sun.
  • 🐾 Animal Week: whales, cheetahs, cobras and snails, plus every other animal in the game library.
  • 🏗️ Big Builds Week: towers, statues, ships and jumbo jets. The Burj Khalifa, the Eiffel Tower, the Titanic and the Boeing 747.
  • 🏏 Sports Week: bats, balls, pitches and the fastest people alive, including Usain Bolt and a Formula 1 car.
  • 🏠 Everyday Week: phones, doors, bodies and the maths of a day. Surprisingly hard, because we think we already know these.

Tips

  • Warm up first. The Weekly uses the same objects, racers and numbers as the Dailies, so a few rounds of Size It Up or Speed Race sharpen your eye for it.
  • Read the comparison word. "Taller", "longer" and "wider" are different questions, and the theme doesn't change that.
  • In Ballpark rounds, count the digits. Ask whether the answer has three digits or four before worrying about the exact number.
  • There's no clock. You only get one try, so take your time on every round.

Play it with friends

Because everyone gets the same ten rounds all week, the Weekly is made for comparing. Share your result and the link becomes a challenge: your friend plays the same set and sees the score to beat. Or start a Friends League for your group and compare your Dailies every day as well.

Questions

When does the Weekly Challenge change?

At midnight on Monday morning, your local time.

Can I play last week's challenge?

Not yet. Past Dailies are in the archive, and past weeks may follow.

Is the Weekly Challenge free?

Yes, with no account and nothing to download.

Does everyone get the same questions?

Yes. The ten rounds are built from the date of that week's Monday, so everyone playing that week gets the same set.

What is a good score?

6 out of 10 makes you a Good Guesser, 8 a Sharp Eye and 9 or 10 a Scale Master. A perfect 10 is rare.

Does it count towards my streak?

No. Streaks are for the Dailies. Your weekly results appear in your stats.
