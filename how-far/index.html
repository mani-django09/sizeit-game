    How far is Delhi from Mumbai, really?

    Most of us know cities by how long the train or flight takes, not by how far apart they actually are. How Far? is a free daily distance guessing game that asks for the straight-line distance between two cities: two pairs in India, two in the USA and one around the world.

    The map is deliberately bare, just dots for cities on a grid, so you have to rely on your own sense of the country and the planet. Then the line is drawn, the real distance appears, and you find out whether Chennai really is closer to Kolkata than Delhi is.

    How to play

    1. Read the two cities. The first is marked in blue-grey and the second in orange. Other cities appear as small dots for reference.
    2. Guess the distance. Slide, tap − and +, use the arrow keys, or type a number. Switch between km and miles with the unit button at the top.
    3. Lock in. The straight line appears with the real distance, plus how long a jet would take to fly it.
    4. Score. Within 5% earns the full 200 points, and three times too far or too short earns nothing. Five rounds make 1000.

    Ways to play

    Daily

    Five city pairs, the same for everyone that day: two in India, two in the USA and one somewhere in the world. It resets at midnight your time and keeps your streak going.

    Unlimited

    Fresh pairs every game. Stick to India only, the USA only or the world, or keep the mix, and practise until you can feel the map.

    Challenge a friend

    Share any result and the link becomes a challenge. Your friend plays the exact same five pairs and sees the score to beat.

    How scoring works

    Points depend on how many times too far or too short your guess was, not on how many kilometres you missed by. Being 200 km off on Delhi to Jaipur is a big miss; being 200 km off on London to Sydney is nearly perfect.

    Tips for guessing distances

    • Learn one anchor. Delhi to Mumbai is about 1,150 km in a straight line. Most Indian city pairs can be judged against it.
    • India is about 3,000 km tall. From Srinagar to Thiruvananthapuram is roughly the whole length of the country.
    • The US is about 4,000 km wide. New York to Los Angeles is roughly 3,940 km (2,450 miles) as the crow flies.
    • Straight lines are shorter than roads. A road trip is usually 20 to 40% longer than the distance in this game.
    • The world is 40,000 km around. No two cities on Earth are more than about 20,000 km apart.

    About this game

    How Far? grew out of a family argument about whether Bengaluru is closer to Mumbai or to Chennai. (Chennai, by a long way: about 290 km against 845.) Train times and flight times give us a feel for travel, but they hide the real shape of the map, and a quick game turned out to be the best way to rebuild it.

    The game knows dozens of cities across India, the USA and the rest of the world, each with its latitude and longitude. Distances are never typed in by hand: they are worked out on the curved surface of the Earth every time, so they are exact for the city centres. A jet cruising at about 900 km/h is used for the flight times you see after each answer.

    It runs in your browser with no account and no download, works on phones, and can be added to your home screen to play offline. Your scores and streaks stay on your device.

    Questions

    Is How Far? free?

    Yes. The Daily, Unlimited and challenge links are all free, with nothing to sign up for.

    When does the Daily reset?

    At midnight in your local time. Everyone gets the same five pairs on the same date, so you can compare scores fairly.

    What is a good score?

    550 or more makes you a Good Guesser, 750 a Sharp Eye and 900 a Scale Master. A full 1000 means every guess was within 5%.

    Can I play against my friends?

    Yes. Share your result and your friend plays the same five pairs, or start a Friends League for your WhatsApp group and compare every day.

    Is this road distance or straight-line distance?

    Straight-line distance, also called great-circle distance or "as the crow flies", measured between city centres. Driving distances are always longer.

    Can I play only Indian cities?

    Yes. In Unlimited, choose India only, USA only, World only, or a mix.

    Can I play past puzzles?

    Yes. Every past Daily is in the archive.
