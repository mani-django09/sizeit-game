Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026.

sizeit.games is a set of free browser games. You don't need an account, and we never ask for your email or phone number. A display name is optional and only used where you choose to share it. This page explains the little that is stored or sent, and why.

Stored in your browser

Your scores, streaks, badges, settings (such as kilometres or miles) and a random device ID are kept in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device unless described below. Clearing your browser data deletes them.

Your time zone is read in the browser to pick sensible defaults, such as feet and pounds in the US. It isn't stored or sent anywhere.

Sent when you finish a Daily

To show how your score compares with other players, finishing a Daily sends:

  • the game and the date of the puzzle,
  • your score, and
  • the random device ID, so the same result isn't counted twice.

To slow down cheating, the server also keeps a one-way hash of your IP address combined with the date. The IP address itself is not stored, and because the date is mixed in, the hash can't be used to follow you from one day to the next.

These results are stored with Supabase, our database provider. Puzzles you replay from the archive are compared but never sent.

Friends Leagues

If you create or join a Friends League, we store the league's name and code, the display name you type, and your random device ID, with Supabase. Anyone who has the league's code can see the league name, the members' display names and their Daily points. Device IDs are never shown. Leaving a league deletes your membership. Use a nickname if you prefer.

Challenge links

When you share a result, the link contains your score and, if you typed one, your display name, so your friend can see who challenged them. Anyone you send the link to can see it. Nothing is stored on our side when you share.

Sent when you suggest an object

The Suggest an object form sends only what you type into it. As with scores, the server keeps a one-way hash of your IP address combined with the date, only to limit how many suggestions one network can send in a day.

How the site is used

To see which games people enjoy and fix what doesn't work, the site records simple activity events: which page was opened, when a game ends (the game, the mode and the score), and when a result is shared or a league is used. Each event carries the random device ID, a random visit ID, a short label such as "Android · Chrome · mobile", the website that linked to us (if any) and the country Cloudflare reports for the request. It never includes your name, IP address or exact location. These events are stored with Supabase and only the site owner can see them.

The site also uses Google Analytics, through Google Tag Manager, to count visits. Google Analytics sets its own cookies and follows Google's privacy policy. A content blocker that blocks Google Analytics stops it on this site.

Hosting and fonts

The site is served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, it handles your IP address to deliver pages and protect the site from attacks. Fonts are served from this site, so no font provider sees your visit. Each provider follows its own privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, emails or phone numbers. Display names are optional.
  • No advertising cookies. The only cookies are Google Analytics' visit counters.
  • No selling or sharing of data.

Your choices

Scores from archive puzzles and from Unlimited and Endless modes never go to the leaderboard, though finishing them is counted in the activity log described above. To remove the data on your device, clear this site's data in your browser settings; you then get a new random device ID. To have activity linked to your device ID deleted, email us the ID (shown nowhere on the site, but stored in your browser as sg_device).
