What to write about

🔢 A number looks wrong

Tell us the game, the date of the Daily and the object, and if you can, a link to a source. We check every report and fix mistakes quickly.

🐞 Something broke

Say what you were doing, which game, and your phone or browser. A screenshot helps a lot.

💡 An idea for a game or object

Objects have their own suggestion form, no email needed. Ideas for new games or features are welcome by email.

🏆 Leagues and schools

Running a league for a class, an office or a club? Tell us how it's going and what would make it better.

📰 Press and partnerships

Writing about the games, or want a themed Weekly for an event? Email us with "Press" or "Partnership" in the subject.

🔒 Your data

Want something removed, such as a league name? See the privacy policy, then email us.

Before you write

  • Looking for today's answers? They're on the answers page, for any date.
  • Missed a day? Every past Daily is in the archive.
  • Lost your streak or stats? They're saved in your browser, so clearing site data or switching phones starts them fresh. We can't restore them, because we never store them.
  • Want feet and pounds? Tap the units button at the top of any game.
