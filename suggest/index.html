Suggest an object

Know something that's surprisingly big, tiny, heavy or fast? Tell us, and it may turn up in a future Daily. Things with a clear, published measurement work best: a landmark's height, an animal's typical weight, a vehicle's length.

What makes a great suggestion

  • A surprise. The best objects are ones people get wrong: the Mona Lisa is only 77 cm tall, and an empty Boeing 747 outweighs a blue whale.
  • A clear measurement. Say what to measure: height to the tip, length nose to tail, width across.
  • A source. An official page, an encyclopedia entry or a manufacturer's figure. We check every number before it goes in.
  • Something people know. A famous landmark, an everyday thing or a well-known animal works better than something nobody has seen.
  • Local favourites. Landmarks, foods, vehicles and animals from India and the US are especially welcome.

What happens next

We check every suggestion against a reliable source, draw an original silhouette, and add the best ones to the games. New objects only join Dailies from the day they're added, so past puzzles never change.

Suggestions are anonymous. We don't ask for your name or email, and the form only sends what you type in it. See the privacy policy.
