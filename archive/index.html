Puzzle Archive

Every past Daily, ready to replay.

Missed a day? Play it now

Every game on sizeit.games has a Daily that is the same for everyone and changes at midnight. The archive keeps all of them, so you can catch up on a puzzle you missed or replay one to see if you'd do better the second time.

Archive games are scored and saved like any other, and they show up in your stats. The one difference: a puzzle played after its day doesn't add to your streak. Streaks are for playing on the day.

How to use the archive

  1. Pick a day. The newest is at the top. Days you've already finished show your score; the rest say Play.
  2. Play it like the Daily. You get exactly the puzzles everyone played that day, built from the date.
  3. See how you'd have ranked. Your score is compared with the players who played on the day, and the comparison says "you'd have beaten". Archive scores are never added to the leaderboard.
  4. Head back to today. The bar at the top of the game takes you to today's Daily at any time.

Questions

How far back does the archive go?

To Daily #1, the day the site started. Every Daily since then is here.

Will an old puzzle change if new objects are added?

No. New objects, numbers and racers only join puzzles from the day they're added, so a past Daily always plays exactly as it did on the day.

Is there an archive for the Weekly Challenge?

Not yet. The archive covers the Dailies of every game.

Can I see the answers for a past day?

Yes. The answers page shows every Daily's answers for any date.
