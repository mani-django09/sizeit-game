Daily answers

The answers to every Daily, explained.

How this page works

Every Daily is built from its date, so everyone gets the same puzzles on the same day. This page builds the same puzzles and shows the true answers, with the real sizes, weights, speeds and distances.

Today's answers are hidden until you ask for them, so you don't spoil a game by accident. If you look first, your score is still saved, but it's only fair to say so when you share it.

Pick any date to see that day's answers for every game: the sizes in Size It Up, the weights in Heavier or Lighter, the correct order in Sort It, the right range in Ballpark, the faster racer in Speed Race, the counts in How Many Fit?, the distances in How Far? and the numbers in Number Guess. Each answer links straight to the game, so you can play that day from the archive.

Questions

Should I look before I play?

Only if you're stuck. The fun is in the guess, and the answer screen in each game already shows the real size, weight, speed or distance as soon as you lock in.

Why are the answers "about" and not exact?

Animals and vehicles vary, so the games use a typical adult or a standard model. Landmarks and distances are exact to the published figure.

When do the answers change?

At midnight in your local time, when the new Dailies start.

Can I replay a past day?

Yes. Every past Daily is in the archive. Archive games count in your stats, but not towards your streak.

Where do the numbers come from?

From public references, linked as "Source" after each fact in the games. Animals and vehicles vary, so typical values are used.
