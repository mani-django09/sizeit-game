    A daily number guessing game

    How many bones are in your body? How far away is the Moon? How many seconds are there in a day? You've probably heard most of these numbers before, and almost nobody remembers them. Number Guess is a free daily estimation game that asks five of these questions every day and rewards you for getting close, not for being exactly right.

    It's a gentle workout for the part of your brain that deals with big numbers, small numbers and everything in between. Some answers you'll half-remember from school. Others you'll have to work out on the spot, and a few will genuinely surprise you.

    How to play

    1. Read the question. Each one has a single number for an answer, sometimes with a unit such as km, days or metres.
    2. Make your guess. Drag the slider, tap − and +, use the arrow keys, or just type it. Typing understands shortcuts like 1.5k, 3 million or 2e9.
    3. Lock it in. The number line shows your guess in orange and the real answer in green, along with a short fact.
    4. Five questions, 1000 points. Each question is worth up to 200. Share your score with coloured squares, or send a friend a link to the same five questions.

    How scoring works

    What counts is how many times too high or too low you were, not the raw difference. Guessing 300,000 km for the Moon's distance is a near miss, while being 300 off on the number of bones in your body is a disaster. Because the score uses ratios, small and huge answers are judged the same way.

    Tips for better guesses

    • Get the number of digits right first. Is it in the hundreds, thousands or millions? That decision is worth far more points than the exact figure.
    • Work it out when you can. Seconds in a day is just 24 × 60 × 60. A surprising number of questions give in to a bit of quick multiplication.
    • Use what you know. If you remember that light from the Sun takes about eight minutes, you're already close to the answer in seconds.
    • Don't over-correct. When a number feels too big, it's often right. Big things in space and time are bigger than our gut expects.

    Questions

    Is Number Guess free?

    Yes. Daily, Unlimited and challenges are all free, with no sign-up and nothing to download.

    When is there a new daily?

    Every day at midnight, your local time. Everyone gets the same five questions on the same date.

    What are the categories in Unlimited?

    Human body, animals and nature, space, Earth and the world, India, sports and games, and everyday maths. Pick one, or play them all mixed together.

    Where do the answers come from?

    Every answer comes from a well-established public source, such as official surveys, scientific references and the rules of each sport. We stick to facts that don't change from month to month.

    Are there other games like this?

    On this site you can also play How Big?, where you resize objects to their real size, and Heavier or Lighter, where you guess which thing weighs more.
