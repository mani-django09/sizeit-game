How leagues work

How do points work?

The same as Today's total: every Daily is worth up to 100 points, so a full day of games is worth 800. The table shows today and the last seven days.

Do my friends need an account?

No. They open the invite link, type a name and join. Their Daily scores count from then on, including today's.

What do other members see?

Only the name you chose and your points. Nothing else about you is stored or shown.

I play on two phones. Does that work?

A league remembers the browser you joined from. Join from each browser with the same name if you like, but only the one you play on will score.
