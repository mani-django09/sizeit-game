About this category

You handle these things every day, which is exactly why guessing their size is harder than it sounds. A phone, a coin, a sheet of paper and a door all feel familiar, yet most people are off by a surprising margin once they have to draw one to scale.

Sizes are standard or typical figures, such as the ISO paper sizes and common door heights, with a public source linked after each answer.

Questions

How does the everyday objects Daily work?

You get five pairs from this category, the same for everyone that day. The grey object is drawn at its real size and you resize the orange one until the pair looks right. A green outline shows the truth, and each round is worth up to 200 points. A new set arrives at midnight, your time.

Where do the sizes come from?

From public references such as official pages and standard encyclopaedia figures. Every answer in the game links to its source after you lock in your guess.

Can I practise before the Daily?

Yes. Unlimited gives you fresh everyday objects pairs as many times as you like, with no score pressure.
