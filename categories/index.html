Size It Up by Category

Pick a topic. Each has its own Daily, five rounds, new at midnight.

A Daily for every topic

The main Size It Up Daily mixes everything: a whale one round, a cricket ball the next. Category Dailies keep to one world at a time. Dinosaur fans get five dinosaur pairs, space fans get planets and rockets, food lovers get mangoes, rotis and pizzas. Every category has its own puzzle each day, the same for everyone who plays it.

The rules are the same as Size It Up: the grey object is drawn at its real size, you resize the orange one until the pair looks right, and a green outline shows the truth. Five rounds, up to 200 points each.

Daily or Unlimited?

Category Daily

Five pairs from one category, the same for everyone that day. One try, a new set at midnight your time. Your score shows on this page once you've played.

Category Unlimited

Fresh pairs from the same category as many times as you like. The best way to get a feel for a topic before the Daily.

Questions

Do category Dailies count towards my streak?

No. Streaks and Today's total come from the main Daily of each game. Category Dailies are extra puzzles for the topics you like most.

Are category scores on the leaderboard?

No, only the main Daily is compared with other players. You can still share a category result: the link gives your friend the exact same five pairs and your score to beat.

Why do some categories mix sizes so much?

Some topics are like that. Space has planets thousands of kilometres across and rockets about a hundred metres tall, so each pair is chosen to be close enough to compare by eye.

Can I suggest a new category?

Yes. Suggest an object and say which topic it belongs to. A category opens once it has enough well-sourced objects.
