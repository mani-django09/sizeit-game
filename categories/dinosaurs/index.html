About this category

Dinosaurs in films are rarely the right size. The real Velociraptor was about the size of a turkey, while the biggest plant-eaters were longer than a tennis court. This category lets you guess dinosaur against dinosaur and then shows the real size drawn to scale.

Dinosaur sizes come from fossils, so they are estimates that scientists keep refining. We use commonly quoted figures and link a source after every answer.

A few things worth knowing

  • Movies made it huge, but Velociraptor was only about 2 m long, tail included, and weighed about 15 kg.
  • Large male Pteranodon had wings about 6 m from tip to tip. Strictly, it was a flying reptile, not a dinosaur.
  • Triceratops grew to about 8 to 9 m long. Weight estimates range from about 6 to 12 t.

Questions

How does the dinosaur Daily work?

You get five pairs from this category, the same for everyone that day. The grey object is drawn at its real size and you resize the orange one until the pair looks right. A green outline shows the truth, and each round is worth up to 200 points. A new set arrives at midnight, your time.

Where do the sizes come from?

From public references such as official pages and standard encyclopaedia figures. Every answer in the game links to its source after you lock in your guess.

Can I practise before the Daily?

Yes. Unlimited gives you fresh dinosaur pairs as many times as you like, with no score pressure.
