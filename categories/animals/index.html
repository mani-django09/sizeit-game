About this category

Animals are the easiest things to picture and the hardest to size. Most people know a giraffe is tall and a whale is huge, but not by how much. In this category every round puts two animals side by side at their real size, and you resize the orange one until the pair looks right.

Sizes are typical values for an adult of the species, with a public source linked after each answer. Animals vary, so we use the commonly quoted figure.

A few things worth knowing

  • A house cat is about 75 cm long, and around 30 cm of that is tail.
  • A bull bison stands up to about 1.8 m (6 ft) at the shoulder and weighs up to about 900 kg (2,000 lb).
  • The greater one-horned rhino of Assam stands about 1.75 to 2 m at the shoulder. Males weigh around 2.2 t.

Questions

How does the animal Daily work?

You get five pairs from this category, the same for everyone that day. The grey object is drawn at its real size and you resize the orange one until the pair looks right. A green outline shows the truth, and each round is worth up to 200 points. A new set arrives at midnight, your time.

Where do the sizes come from?

From public references such as official pages and standard encyclopaedia figures. Every answer in the game links to its source after you lock in your guess.

Can I practise before the Daily?

Yes. Unlimited gives you fresh animal pairs as many times as you like, with no score pressure.
