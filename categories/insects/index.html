About this category

Insects range from a couple of millimetres to wingspans wider than your hand. Drawing them at true scale is a good reminder of how small an ant is next to a bee, and how big the biggest moths get.

Sizes are typical adult measurements, and a source is linked after each answer. Bugs vary, so we use the commonly quoted figure.

A few things worth knowing

  • A black garden ant worker is only about 3 to 5 mm long.
  • The seven-spot ladybird is about 5 to 8 mm long.
  • A worker honeybee is about 12 to 15 mm long and weighs about a tenth of a gram.

Questions

How does the insect Daily work?

You get five pairs from this category, the same for everyone that day. The grey object is drawn at its real size and you resize the orange one until the pair looks right. A green outline shows the truth, and each round is worth up to 200 points. A new set arrives at midnight, your time.

Where do the sizes come from?

From public references such as official pages and standard encyclopaedia figures. Every answer in the game links to its source after you lock in your guess.

Can I practise before the Daily?

Yes. Unlimited gives you fresh insect pairs as many times as you like, with no score pressure.
