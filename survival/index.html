How long can you last?

Bigger or smaller, heavier or lighter, faster, farther apart, more or less. Each question is a choice between two, and the clock is ticking. A wrong answer or running out of time costs a life. The questions get closer and the clock gets shorter the longer you survive.

An endless estimation quiz

Survival mixes every kind of question on the site into one fast run. Is a blue whale longer than a Boeing 747? Is a Velociraptor heavier than a greyhound? Are Delhi and Mumbai farther apart than New York and Chicago? Answer before the bar runs out.

You start with three lives. Every wrong answer, and every question you let time out, costs one. At first the two answers are far apart and you have 14 seconds. As your score grows, the answers get closer together and the clock drops to 7 seconds.

The five kinds of question

  • 📏 Size: which is taller, longer or wider?
  • ⚖️ Weight: which is heavier?
  • 🏁 Speed: which is faster?
  • 🗺️ Distance: which two cities are farther apart?
  • 🔢 Number: is the real number more or less than the one shown?

Press 1 or 2 to answer and Enter to move on.

Tips for lasting longer

  • Don't let the clock answer for you. A timeout costs a life just like a wrong answer, so when you're unsure, pick the one that feels likelier and move on.
  • Read the comparison word. "Taller", "longer" and "wider" are different questions. A blue whale is far longer than a giraffe, but nowhere near as tall.
  • Heavy is denser than it looks. Metal and stone beat living things of the same size: an empty Boeing 747 outweighs a blue whale.
  • Animal speeds are sprints. A house cat at full tilt beats Usain Bolt, but only for a few seconds.
  • Practise in the other games. Every question comes from the same data as the Dailies, so Size It Up, Heavier or Lighter, Speed Race, How Far? and Number Guess all sharpen your Survival runs.

About Survival

Survival is the site's arcade mode. There's no Daily and no limit: every run is a fresh shuffle of all five kinds of question, dealt in rotation so no kind goes missing for long. It's the game to play when you've finished your Dailies and still want more, or when you want to settle an argument with a friend right now.

It's free, runs in your browser, works on phones with big tap targets, and switches between metric and imperial with the unit button. Your best score and number of runs are saved on your device.

Questions

Is there a Daily Survival?

No. Survival is endless, so you can play as many runs as you like. Your best score is saved on this device and shows in your stats.

Can I challenge a friend?

Yes. Share your result and the link tells your friend exactly what to beat. Runs are random, so they get different questions, but the same rules and clock.

Does a timeout cost a life?

Yes. Running out of time counts the same as a wrong answer and is shown as ⏱️ in your share.

Is Survival free?

Yes, every run, with no sign-up and no limit.

Are there badges for Survival?

Yes. Reach 10 to earn Survivor and 25 to earn Unbreakable. See all badges on the stats page.
