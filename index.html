Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Every game runs in your browser for free, with no account and no download.

Where do the sizes and weights come from?

From public references such as official landmark pages, the Laws of Cricket and manufacturer specifications. Every fact in the games links to its source. Animals and vehicles vary, so we use typical values.

What is Today's total?

Every Daily is worth up to 100 points, so the eight games add up to a total out of 800. Share it to show how your whole day went, and come back tomorrow to beat it.

Can I play against my friends?

Yes. Start a Friends League, send its code to your WhatsApp group, and everyone's Daily scores add up on one table, today and for the week. No account needed: just a name. You can also share any result as a challenge link, and your friend plays the exact same puzzles.

Can I play in feet and pounds?

Yes. Press the units button at the top of any game to switch between metres and kilograms or feet and pounds. Players in the US start in feet and pounds.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Every game works on phones, and you can add the site to your home screen to use it like an app.

Will there be more games?

Yes. New games are added regularly, and every game gets a Daily so there's always something fresh.

Who makes sizeit.games?

sizeit.games is an independent site. Every game, drawing and puzzle here is our own, and every number links to a public source.
