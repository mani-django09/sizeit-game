    How to play the food size game

    From a single grain of rice to a whole pizza, food is full of sizes we never really measure. This category pairs small things with big things and asks you to resize one until it matches the other.

    Food varies a lot from one to the next, so we use typical sizes, with a source linked after each answer.

    It plays like Size It Up, with every round from this category. You can also browse every category.

    A few things worth knowing

    • An uncooked basmati grain is about 7 mm long and weighs about 0.02 g. It can nearly double in length when cooked.
    • A large hen's egg is about 5.5 to 6 cm tall. In the US, a dozen large eggs must weigh at least 24 oz, about 57 g each.
    • A medium apple is about 7.5 cm across and weighs about 180 g.

    Questions

    How does the food Daily work?

    You get five pairs from this category, the same for everyone that day. The grey object is drawn at its real size and you resize the orange one until the pair looks right. A green outline shows the truth, and each round is worth up to 200 points. A new set arrives at midnight, your time.

    Where do the sizes come from?

    From public references such as official pages and standard encyclopaedia figures. Every answer in the game links to its source after you lock in your guess.

    Can I practise before the Daily?

    Yes. Unlimited gives you fresh food pairs as many times as you like, with no score pressure.

    How to play

    1. Each round shows two objects. The grey one is drawn at its real size.
    2. Resize the orange one until it looks right next to it. Drag on the board, use the slider, or press the arrow keys.
    3. Press Lock in. The green outline shows the real size.
    4. Within 5% earns the full 200 points. Five rounds, 1000 points.
    5. A new Daily unlocks at midnight. Unlimited never runs out, and you can pick a category.
